# Ultima IV original-data hosting evidence

Rechecked 2026-09-15. This is a source-grounded project decision, not a legal
opinion or a claim that no separate permission could ever be obtained.

The current [GOG Ultima IV product page](https://www.gog.com/en/game/ultima_iv_quest_of_the_avatar)
offers the game free, identifies Electronic Arts, and links an EA EULA under
its system requirements. Free acquisition and DRM-free packaging do not, on
their own, authorize a third party to mirror game data.

The directly linked [EA Software End User License Agreement](https://items.gog.com/EULA.pdf)
grants personal non-commercial installation/use in section 1.A but prohibits
redistribution. Section 1.C also prohibits network availability/downloads for
multiple users without express EA authorization. No general hosting permission
was found in this linked license. It is the license currently linked by GOG,
not proof of the terms supplied with every historical edition.

[Contrapuntal Dragon's original download page](https://ultima.thatfleminggent.com/u4download.html)
describes a specific Origin permission for Ultima Dragons and says the game
cannot be further redistributed without permission. The preserved
[Mike McCoy/Origin correspondence](https://ultima.thatfleminggent.com/boomer.txt)
from October 1997 approves Dragons redistributing the CGW-CD version. That is
historical, scoped evidence—not a blanket current permission for Ultimatum
Project, every game-data edition, or account-based hosting.

Archive.org availability, other mirrors and freeware labels are not an
independent copyright-owner license. The user's archive is a compatibility
fixture only; passing hashes does not establish redistribution permission.

Decision: keep public gameplay bring-your-own-data and link to GOG for free
acquisition. Do not mirror or preload original DOS game data without an
applicable express permission. The open-source engine's distribution is a
separate question governed by its own license/source obligations. No contact
with EA, permission request, third-party account action or public deployment
has been made in this work. Private account storage is not certified lawful
by this narrow original-data-mirroring review.
